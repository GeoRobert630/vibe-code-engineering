# Privacy & Compliance

Inventory:
- personal data collected
- purpose
- storage
- retention
- recipients/processors
- tracking/analytics
- cookies
- forms
- third-party services

Check for:
- privacy policy
- cookie policy/notice where appropriate
- consent mechanisms where required
- data minimization
- unnecessary tracking
- unsupported compliance claims

Do not present this as legal advice. Identify gaps and items for qualified legal review.
