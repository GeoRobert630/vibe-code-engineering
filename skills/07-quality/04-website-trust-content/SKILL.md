# Website Trust & Content

Check:
- real business/contact details
- Terms & Conditions
- privacy information
- refund/cancellation policy where applicable
- truthful claims
- removal of fake or unsupported reviews
- image/content rights
- third-party embeds
- tracking disclosure
- clear calls to action
- form consent

Do not invent business claims, reviews, certifications or legal statements.
