# Requirements Review

## Objective

Stop premature coding.

Before implementation, identify what must exist, who uses it, what data it handles, and which rules must always hold.

## Required questions

- What is the feature/product?
- Who are the actors?
- What roles exist?
- What can each role view/create/update/delete?
- What data is sensitive?
- What are the business rules?
- What external services are involved?
- What are success and failure conditions?
- What abuse/misuse cases exist?
- What must be logged?
- What is explicitly out of scope?

## Deliverables

Create:

1. Functional requirements
2. Non-functional requirements
3. Roles and permissions
4. Data entities
5. Acceptance criteria
6. Abuse cases
7. Security requirements
8. Test requirements
9. External dependencies
10. Open questions

## Rule

Do not silently invent missing business rules. Mark assumptions and unresolved questions explicitly.
